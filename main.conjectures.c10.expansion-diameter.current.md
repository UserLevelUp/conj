# C10 - Joint cycle-conditioned fluctuations of mixing and tree entropy

**Status:** high-risk candidate conjecture; truth, exact novelty, and open status unverified.

**Definitions.** Use $G_n,d,\mathcal C_n,T_n$ from C09, and an independent
uniform root $o$. A nonbacktracking walk chooses its first edge uniformly
and subsequently chooses uniformly among edges other than its preceding
edge. Let $P_t(o,\cdot)$ be its endpoint law, conditional on $G_n$.
Set $V_n=\frac12\sum_v|P_{t_n}(o,v)-1/n|$.
Take integer $t_n$ along any subsequence with
$d(d-1)^{t_n-1}/n\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)$.
Let $\mathcal F_n$ be exactly the sigma-field generated by $\mathcal C_n$,
$o$, and its labeled induced radius
$\lfloor(\log n)/(4\log(d-1))\rfloor$ neighborhood, with no additional
graph information. Work under the law conditioned on this neighborhood
being a tree.

Center and standardize $T_n$ by its conditional mean and standard deviation
given $\mathcal F_n$. For $V_n$, let $q_\beta$ be its conditional
$\beta$ quantile (the infimum where its CDF reaches $\beta$), and define
$s_V=(q_{3/4}-q_{1/4})/(2\Phi^{-1}(3/4))$.
Use the residual $(V_n-q_{1/2})/s_V$; $\Phi$ is the normal CDF.

**Proposal.** The entropy variance and $s_V$ are positive with probability tending to one,
and the conditional joint law of the two standardized residuals converges
in bounded-Lipschitz distance, in probability, to $N(0,I_2)$.
The target is Gaussian fluctuation independence of a dynamical cutoff
observable and global spanning-tree entropy after shared local information,
not a collision-probability upper bound.

**Analytic checks performed.** There are exactly $d(d-1)^{t-1}$ potential
walk paths; the specified subsequence fixes their number per vertex at a
nonzero finite scale. At radius $\log_{d-1}n/4$, the usual collision
first-moment scale is $n^{-1/2}$, making the tree conditioning nonvacuous.
These are only path-count and conditioning checks. Adaptive pairing
collisions are not independent trials. Rare early collisions can contribute
disproportionately to a second moment, so the mixing claim uses conditional
quantiles and does not assert that its exact variance calibrates its typical
fluctuations.

**Alignment/eventness.** Compare residual mixing and entropy fluctuations;
the proposed independence is a specified distributional alignment target.
The event is entering the nonbacktracking cutoff window.

**Context/falsification.** Random-regular nonbacktracking cutoff and
small-subgraph conditioning provide background. This is particularly
vulnerable to non-Gaussian rare histories or a shared residual mode.
Calculate conditional quantiles, entropy variance, and mixed tail probabilities before attempting a
proof. No computation supports the joint Gaussian claim yet.
