# C13 · A transversal that survives damage

**Combinatorial resilience · Priority · unresolved**

Can every locally bounded deletion attack leave a transversal in a random Latin square?

![Constructed order-seven cyclic square: one deletion per row, column, and symbol, with a surviving transversal. Not a random-square test.](../../assets/c13.png)

*Figure provenance: Constructed order-seven cyclic square: one deletion per row, column, and symbol, with a surviving transversal. Not a random-square test.* [Vector version](../../assets/c13.svg).

The adversary sees the square, but may delete fewer than one third of the cells at each row, column, and symbol. The random ensemble is essential.
