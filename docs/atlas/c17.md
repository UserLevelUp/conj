# C17 · The Gaussian shape of missing sums

**Additive probability · Priority · unresolved**

Do sumset deficits converge to a Gaussian field with an explicit covariance kernel?

![Recorded finite simulations: full-deficit scaled sample variances and projection residual variances. The dashed 8 is the conjectured limit.](../../assets/c17.png)

*Figure provenance: Recorded finite simulations: full-deficit scaled sample variances and projection residual variances. The dashed 8 is the conjectured limit.* [Vector version](../../assets/c17.svg).

A first-order projection predicts the kernel and full-deficit variance constant 8. Recorded experiments support further investigation; controlling the higher-order remainder is the missing mathematical step.
