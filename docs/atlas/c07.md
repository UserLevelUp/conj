# C07 · Two edges after a graph is thinned

**Random graph spectra · Priority · unresolved**

Do the upper and lower nontrivial spectral edges approach independent Tracy–Widom laws?

![A small regular graph, its vertex-thinned induced graph, and the actual normalized toy spectrum; no Tracy–Widom fit.](../../assets/c07.png)

*Figure provenance: A small regular graph, its vertex-thinned induced graph, and the actual normalized toy spectrum; no Tracy–Widom fit.* [Vector version](../../assets/c07.svg).

Vertex removal creates irregular degrees. The proposed law conditions on the retained vertices and their degrees, and normalizes using the surviving graph itself.

**Next mathematical step:** Check prescribed-degree universality, edge moments, and possible localized extremes.
