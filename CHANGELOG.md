# Changes

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-12

First explicitly numbered release, following the unversioned public collection
at commit `7acbd81`. The original 25 statements are unchanged.

- Prove the fully observed subset of C15, with an exact simultaneous confidence
  formula and its Gumbel limit. Add a separate missing-data result with a known
  nuisance matrix; it does not resolve the unknown-nuisance conjecture.
- Derive a conditional decision rule requiring both positive expected net
  return and a bounded probability of endpoint loss. State the prospective
  assumptions, repeated-look correction, and selection limitations explicitly.
- Add reproducible numerical checks, an analytic figure, and Recur notebook
  lineage. These are standard consequences, not claims of novel theorems or a
  demonstrated market forecasting advantage.
